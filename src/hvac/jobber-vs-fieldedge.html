Jobber vs FieldEdge for HVAC (2026)

Jobber and FieldEdge represent two eras of HVAC software. Jobber is the modern, self-serve, all-round platform with a free trial and a polished client portal. FieldEdge's parent company was founded by HVAC contractors in 1980; the current cloud product dates from 2016. It's built around QuickBooks Desktop and flat-rate pricebooks, sold by quote and demo. Which one fits comes down to a single question more than any other: do you run QuickBooks Desktop, and do you refuse to move off it? If yes, FieldEdge has a genuine edge. If not, Jobber is usually the easier, cheaper, more flexible choice.

The Short Answer

Choose Jobber if you want a fast, low-risk start (14-day free trial, no card), the strongest client self-service portal, well-rated iOS and Android apps (4.8 and 4.4), native US + Canada coverage, and multi-trade flexibility — all at transparent pricing from $49/mo. This fits most HVAC shops.

Choose FieldEdge if you're an established shop that runs QuickBooks Desktop and won't migrate, and you want flat-rate pricebooks (FieldEdge Flat Rate). Its two-way Desktop accounting sync is a real advantage Jobber can't match — but it comes with quote-only pricing, a demo-first sales process, and no free trial.

Where Jobber Wins

Ease, transparency, and flexibility. Jobber lets you start free with no card, see exactly what you'll pay, and be running in days — versus FieldEdge's demo-gated quote. Its client self-service portal (approve quotes, track jobs, pay online) is clearly stronger, its mobile apps rate 4.8 (iOS) and 4.4 (Android), and it's a natural fit for Canadian shops and multi-trade businesses. For a shop that just wants a modern, capable HVAC platform without friction, Jobber is the easier yes.

Where FieldEdge Wins

QuickBooks Desktop and flat-rate pricing. This is narrow but real. If your accounting lives on QuickBooks Desktop, FieldEdge's two-way sync eliminates double entry in a way Jobber (which syncs with QuickBooks Online, not Desktop) simply can't. Add FieldEdge Flat Rate for consistent in-field pricing, and FieldEdge is purpose-built for the established HVAC shop that has run this way for years and doesn't want to change its accounting. Its Capterra customer-service score is 4.4/5.

The Deciding Question

Are you committed to QuickBooks Desktop? If yes, and you value flat-rate pricebooks, FieldEdge's advantages are concrete — trial it via demo. If you use QuickBooks Online, some other accounting tool, or nothing yet, then FieldEdge's signature strength doesn't apply to you, and Jobber's easier setup, transparent pricing, better portal and apps, and free trial make it the stronger default. Don't pay for a QuickBooks Desktop integration you won't use.

Which Should You Pick?

Pick Jobber if: you want a free trial and transparent pricing; you value the client portal and strong mobile apps; you operate in Canada; you run multiple trades; or you're not tied to QuickBooks Desktop. This is most HVAC shops.

Pick FieldEdge if: you're an established shop on QuickBooks Desktop that won't migrate, and flat-rate pricebooks matter to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jobber or FieldEdge better for HVAC?

For most HVAC shops, Jobber — it's easier to start (free trial, no card), transparently priced from $49/mo, has a strong client portal and well-rated mobile apps, and is headquartered in Canada. FieldEdge is better for one specific profile: an established shop that runs QuickBooks Desktop and won't move off it, and wants FieldEdge Flat Rate pricebooks. FieldEdge's two-way QuickBooks Desktop sync is a genuine advantage Jobber can't match, but it only matters if you actually use QuickBooks Desktop.

Does Jobber sync with QuickBooks Desktop?

No — Jobber integrates with QuickBooks Online, not QuickBooks Desktop. This is FieldEdge's signature advantage: it offers two-way sync with QuickBooks Desktop, which eliminates double entry for shops that keep their books on Desktop. If you run QuickBooks Desktop and won't migrate to Online, that's a strong reason to favor FieldEdge. If you're on QuickBooks Online or open to it, Jobber's integration covers you and the advantage disappears.

Which is cheaper, Jobber or FieldEdge?

Jobber is cheaper to start and more transparent — Core is $49/mo for 1 user, Connect is $139/mo for 1 user or $199/mo for 5, and Grow is $399/mo for 10, month to month, with a 14-day free trial. FieldEdge is quote only and doesn't publish prices, so you can't compare without a sales call. Jobber lets you test before paying.
