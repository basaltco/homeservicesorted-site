Housecall Pro vs ServiceTitan for HVAC (2026)

This is a comparison between two tools built for very different companies. Housecall Pro is designed for HVAC shops with roughly 1–20 technicians and prices and behaves accordingly. ServiceTitan is enterprise software built for 20+ truck operations, and in a 2025 response to a BBB complaint, ServiceTitan said its software isn't optimized for businesses with fewer than three technicians. So the real question isn't "which is better" in the abstract — it's "which is right for a business your size." For most HVAC shops searching this comparison, the answer is Housecall Pro, and we'll explain exactly when it isn't.

The Short Answer

Choose Housecall Pro if you have fewer than about 20 technicians. You'll get the workflow HVAC shops actually need — dispatch, maintenance-plan automation, invoicing, online booking, financing — at roughly $79–$329/month, live within days, with no enterprise implementation. Its Service Plans feature (MAX plan or paid add-on) auto-bills and auto-schedules maintenance agreements, which is the core of HVAC recurring revenue.

Choose ServiceTitan only if you're a larger operation (20+ techs) with dedicated admin staff and the budget for an enterprise contract. It's far more powerful — deeper dispatch, reporting, and marketing analytics — but that power is wasted, and its cost punishing, at small-shop scale.

Where Housecall Pro Wins

Cost, speed, and simplicity for normal-sized shops. For an HVAC business under 20 techs, Housecall Pro delivers what you need at a fraction of the cost and complexity. You can sign up, configure it, and be dispatching within days — no enterprise implementation project. Its Service Plans feature handles the maintenance-agreement recurring revenue that matters most to HVAC, and it's genuinely usable by non-technical staff. For the overwhelming majority of HVAC shops, this is the right tool.

Where ServiceTitan Wins

Depth at scale. Once you're running 20+ trucks with multiple divisions, ServiceTitan's advanced dispatch, capacity planning, granular reporting, and marketing-attribution tools do things Housecall Pro doesn't attempt. Large operations use it to see exactly which techs and campaigns are profitable and to manage memberships at scale. If you have the size and the admin capacity, that depth can drive real growth — the cost and implementation are the price of admission.

The Cost Reality

The gap is stark at small-shop scale. A 5-technician HVAC business pays $189/month on Housecall Pro Essentials month to month, or $329/month on MAX if it wants Service Plans included. ServiceTitan doesn't publish prices, so you won't know its number until you get a quote — per technician — and ask whether implementation is extra. For a shop that size, Housecall Pro's published price is the safer bet. Buy the platform that fits your size today, not the one you imagine needing at four times your current headcount.

Which Should You Pick?

Pick Housecall Pro if: you have under ~20 techs; you want maintenance-plan automation without complexity; you need to be running quickly; or cost and ease of use matter. This is most HVAC shops.

Pick ServiceTitan if: you're 20+ techs with dedicated admin staff, you need enterprise dispatch/reporting/marketing depth, and you have the budget for an enterprise contract.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan better for HVAC?

For most HVAC shops — those under about 20 technicians — Housecall Pro is better: it delivers dispatch, maintenance-plan automation, invoicing, and financing at a fraction of the cost, and you can be running in days. ServiceTitan is more powerful but built for 20+ tech operations with dedicated admin staff and a budget for an enterprise contract. In a 2025 response to a BBB complaint, ServiceTitan said its software isn't optimized for businesses with fewer than three technicians. Match the tool to your size: small-to-mid shop → Housecall Pro; large operation → ServiceTitan.

How much cheaper is Housecall Pro than ServiceTitan?

ServiceTitan doesn't publish prices, so there's no list-price comparison. A 5-technician HVAC business pays $189/month on Housecall Pro Essentials month to month, or $329/month on MAX, which includes Service Plans. ServiceTitan quotes per technician; get a written quote at your headcount, including implementation, before you compare. Its cost only becomes defensible when its efficiency and revenue gains at scale exceed the spend — realistic for large operations, rarely for small ones.

When should an HVAC company move from Housecall Pro to ServiceTitan?

Consider the move when you're approaching or past roughly 20 technicians, you have dedicated dispatch and admin staff, you're running multiple divisions (install and service), and you're hitting real limits in Housecall Pro's reporting and capacity planning. At that point ServiceTitan's depth can pay for itself. Below that scale — or without the admin capacity to run it — you'll pay enterprise prices for features you can't fully use. Many shops never need to switch.
